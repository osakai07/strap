How to connect / debug log
  1. Open this page in Bluefy on your iPhone.
  2. Close the official WHOOP app (swipe it away) so it doesn't hold the connection.
  3. Tap Connect WHOOP, pick your strap. If iOS asks to pair, accept.
  4. If no heart rate shows after ~5 s, tap Start HR stream.
  5. Keep the page open; iOS stops Bluetooth when the browser goes to the background.
